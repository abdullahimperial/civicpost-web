Safety and responsible use

CivicPost Canada · 2026-11-14

Test-build notice: public release details and policy review are pending.

Check before sending

  • Confirm the office, address, current officeholder and jurisdiction on an official source. Source dates and expiry warnings matter.
  • Postal results are possible matches. A postal code or approximate city does not prove your ward, riding, school board or Indigenous affiliation.
  • Check names, dates and facts. Keep the message truthful, respectful and specific. Imported campaign drafts may be inaccurate or biased; edit them in your own words.
  • Do not include passwords, bank details, identity documents, health records, children's details or a home address unless necessary and appropriate for the receiving office.
  • Review To and Cc: copied addresses are visible to recipients. Shared drafts, links and QR codes are readable by anyone who obtains them.

Urgent matters and official processes

CivicPost is not monitored for emergencies and does not contact emergency services. For immediate danger in Canada, call 911. For a legal deadline, formal complaint, application or appeal, use the required official channel and confirm receipt. Sending an ordinary email may not meet legal requirements.

External links and advertising

Check a link's destination before providing personal information. Ad links are not government services and are not endorsements. Never pay an advertiser believing it is a required CivicPost step. All CivicPost drafting features work without viewing or interacting with ads.

Report a problem

Use the support contact below to report inaccurate source information or an app/privacy issue. Include the office/source link and app version, but avoid sending your private letter or sensitive personal information unless needed. For a threat or urgent safety concern, use the appropriate emergency or official service.
