Privacy policy

CivicPost Canada · 2026-11-21

Test-build notice: public release details and policy review are pending.

CivicPost helps you prepare messages to Canadian public offices. You do not create a CivicPost account. This public website requires no sign-in. The Android app is a separate bundled edition.

Website agreement

If you agree to the terms, CivicPost stores the version you accepted in this browser. You will be asked again when the notice or policies change or when you clear saved app data. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice lasts for this visit.

Your drafts and location

Names, concerns, recipients and draft text are processed on your device using local templates, not an AI service. Unsaved text stays in the current app session. Saving a draft stores up to ten drafts in this browser or app profile until you delete them. Remember location is optional and stores your chosen location until removed. A saved postal lookup stores one code and public representative data for up to 30 days; it is discarded when next read after expiry. App storage is not separately encrypted by CivicPost. Someone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to read it. The Android app disables Android backup.

When information leaves your device

  • Choosing Play here in Help connects to YouTube and Google to stream the video. They receive network and playback information, including your IP address. CivicPost sends only the app or website origin as the referrer and does not send your draft or office selections. You can also open the video or channel on YouTube.
  • The website downloads geographic files from its static host. Opening a campaign sends its public campaign ID to the contact service to retrieve its provincial office. Contact searches use a separate Cloudflare service, which receives your IP address and the lookup fields you choose, including community, districts, school board, issue category and office search terms. Your name and letter text are not sent to that service. Lookup frequency is limited; contact responses are kept only in this tab’s memory, not the offline cache. The application does not write request logs, although hosting providers process requests under their own policies. Explicitly saved drafts and exported files can contain chosen contacts and remain separate. Offline contact searches are unavailable; previously saved geographic files and drafting can still work. Postal lookup then checks Open North Represent for updates. That request sends the postal code and exposes your network IP address and standard request metadata. Your name and letter are not sent. You can choose a location manually to avoid this request.
  • Suggest my city sends a request to BigDataCloud, which uses your IP address to estimate your city. GPS, your name and letter are not sent. Manual location entry avoids that request.
  • Opening a mail draft hands the recipients, subject and message to your selected email app or Gmail/Outlook website. Those services then handle the information under their own policies. Pressing Send is a separate action in that service. Offices receiving an email may retain it or disclose it under applicable access-to-information laws.
  • QR codes, share links and exported draft files contain the message, recipient addresses and included location details. Anyone who obtains them can read or forward them. Link payloads are in the URL fragment; this is not encryption. Files, screenshots, clipboard copies and emails remain outside CivicPost after export.
  • Official source and service links open external sites. They receive ordinary web request information. The hosted website provider may process network/device and sign-in data under its own policy. The Android app bundles its pages and does not require visiting that host.

Website visit analytics

This public website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure visits, page paths, referring sites, browser/device information, approximate country and loading performance. It does not use analytics cookies or identify returning individuals. Cloudflare receives network request information, including your IP address; its documentation states the IP is discarded at its edge and is not stored in its core analytics databases or logs. CivicPost sends no custom analytics events, letter text, names, selected recipients or form values. Query strings and shared-draft fragments are excluded from the tested beacon payload. Analytics availability depends on your browser and blockers; visits are not a count of unique people or emails sent. The separate Android app does not include this beacon. See Cloudflare analytics data and privacy.

Advertising and consent

The offline Android edition has no advertising or analytics SDK. This public website has no advertising SDK and uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, described below. An optional Android AdMob edition offers ads only in its separate About & guide screen, after an adult user chooses to enable them. All drafting features work without ads. Google UMP manages available consent and privacy choices before Google Mobile Ads is initialized. Even non-personalized ads may involve device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics and fraud-prevention data. CivicPost does not pass your letter, selected offices, issue, name or postal code to the ad SDK. The ad SDK is not connected to the WebView content. Google may process data in other countries under its policies. An ad is not an endorsement by CivicPost or a public office.

In the AdMob edition, open Advertising choices to manage available Google privacy options or turn ads off. Turning off ads prevents future app-initiated ad requests; it does not erase data already handled by Google. The test installer has no ad SDK. Declining optional ads never blocks the app.

Camera and files

On compatible web browsers, QR scanning requests camera permission only when you start scanning; frames are decoded locally. In Android, choose an image or draft through the system document picker. CivicPost does not request access to your contacts, microphone or precise GPS. It can read files you choose and write exports to locations you select.

Deletion, retention and security

Use Menu → Privacy, terms & safety → Delete saved app data to remove CivicPost saved drafts, remembered location, saved lookup and notice acknowledgement, and clear the current letter. Individual saved drafts and lookups can also be removed separately. Browser site-data settings or Android app-storage settings can remove local app data. Clearing app data does not delete previously exported files, shared links, clipboard contents, emails or information retained by outside services. Contact those services for their retention/deletion rules. CivicPost does not operate a server-side draft store. HTTPS is used for app network requests, but no system can promise absolute security. Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information.
