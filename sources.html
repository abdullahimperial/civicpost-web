Government information and sources

CivicPost Canada · 2026-11-14

Test-build notice: public release details and policy review are pending.

CivicPost is independent and does not represent a government entity. Government information is drawn from published directories and source records; it may be outdated or incomplete.

In the app, choose an office's Details & source to see its actual source URL, jurisdiction, retrieval date and any expiry warning. Menu → Coverage & sources describes gaps. Secondary sources are labelled. A retrieval date does not certify an address or prove delivery.

  • House of Commons member directory
  • Senate of Canada directory
  • Government of Canada departments and agencies
  • Elections Canada
  • Open North Represent — independent data service

Provincial, territorial, municipal and school contacts have individual source links in the directory. The bundled geographic database has its own provenance and licences in Offline database sources. No address is generated from an official's name. Unsupported jurisdictions use official finder/service links instead.
