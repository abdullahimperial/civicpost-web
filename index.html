Before you use CivicPost

CivicPost is an independent, nonpartisan drafting tool provided by Abdullah Mohiuddin. It has no government affiliation and is provided “as is.”

Office details and suggested recipients can be wrong or out of date. Check official sources and review your message before you press Send in your email app. Delivery and responses are not guaranteed. Use official channels for emergencies, filings and deadlines.

Your drafts stay on your device until you share them or open them in an email service. Online lookups send the details needed to find offices. The website uses Cloudflare visit analytics. Anyone with a shared draft link or QR code can read its contents.
